## ✨ New Features {#✨-новые-возможности}

- Comments on diagrams and wiki pages have become full-fledged discussions: you can create new threads, reply, edit and delete your messages, mark important branches, and close/reopen discussions.  
- Comments now support mentioning colleagues via «@» with convenient user selection, simplifying approval and engaging the right participants in the discussion.  
- Wiki pages now feature page discussions: you can open a thread for any article, ask questions, leave remarks, and maintain an approval history directly within the document context.  
- New invoice types have been added to CRM: offer invoice and invoice with a link, as well as generating a legal entity invoice for a selected client team — this simplifies working with various billing scenarios.  
- The simulations section now includes templates: you can import ready-made cases into your team with a single click to start process modeling faster.  
- In the process registry and org structure, you can automatically import models, structures, and people from team files — this accelerates the start of work in STORM BPMN when migrating data.  
- The import section now features clear visual blocks about the registry and AI, as well as links from cards — making it easier to understand which data and scenarios can be migrated to the system.  

## 🚀 Improvements {#🚀-улучшения}

- The process list now displays only the process models available to you, while other models are restricted by the Team plan and are neatly consolidated into a single convenient list.
- Working with the org structure is more reliable and faster for large staffing tables: employees and positions are loaded in batches, preventing filter lag.
- In the BPMN task editor, deleting multiple elements and managing task sizes have been improved, reducing the risk of accidental actions when editing process models.
- For models not linked to a team, task descriptions can now be viewed and copied, while editing is available when working in a team — this makes using shared models safer.
- For models without the Team plan, data on roles and architecture elements is displayed as a showcase, providing a general overview of capabilities without access to detailed data.
- Emails from STORM BPMN have received an updated design and a unified style, including for sending via SMTP, making notifications clearer and more professional.
- In the interface for empty states (when there is no data yet), sizes have been aligned and a compact variant with a "Retry" button has been added for loading failures — making it easier to understand what to do next.  

## 🔧 Fixes {#🔧-исправления}

- Improved comment stability: message edits, update timestamps, deletion, mentions, and thread closing are now handled correctly, including during unstable connections.  
- The "Shared with me" section now displays only models actually shared with you, excluding extraneous diagrams from other teams.  
- Fixed rare hangs in the survey editor and situations where an access error could result in a blank white page.  
- Resolved issues with saving employees in filters and org structure elements, preventing "stuck" old values.  
- Adjusted task description handling and multi-element deletion confirmation in the BPMN editor to prevent accidental data loss.  
- Fixed specific inaccuracies in link handling for diagrams with invitations and the return of invited users to the correct diagram after registration.  
- Resolved errors during team link registration and team payment processing to ensure invitations and payments are correctly accounted for without creating duplicates.  
- Corrected minor visual and textual inaccuracies on marketing pages and in the wiki to ensure the interface appears neat and predictable.
