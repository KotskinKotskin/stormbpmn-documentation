## ✨ New Features {#✨-новые-возможности}

- The personal account home page has become a workspace: a quick start via the "Create" button, a first-steps checklist, AI hints, and an achievements block help you get up to speed in STORM BPMN faster.
- Interactive learning tours have been introduced: they do not launch automatically but carefully offer assistance to newcomers and wait for the completion of a step, not just a click.
- Contextual help has been added directly in the interface areas where questions most often arise — you can get an answer without leaving for the documentation.
- The "Company Model" section now features ready-made first-step chains — this helps structure work with processes at the start.
- A catalog of ready-made registries has been added to the personal account: you can browse them, view previews, and import them into your working environment.
- Excel export and import have been added for working with business roles: you can now bulk export roles, edit them in a spreadsheet, and upload them back.
- The diagram editor now features full navigation of the schema on touch devices: panning with a finger and zooming with a "pinch" gesture.
- In the process model, the "With AI" button now opens a new chat with a pre-prepared request — making it easier to formulate a task for the AI.  

## 🚀 Improvements {#🚀-улучшения}

- Large BPMN diagrams open and operate significantly faster: rendering has been optimized, a mini-map has been added, and label placement has been improved.
- The diagram page loads faster: heavy elements are loaded on demand, and the canvas does not wait for auxiliary data.
- The error panel in the diagram is now lighter and more responsive, and the loading indicator is more intuitive.
- Labels on diagrams at large scales are more readable: text and roles are visually more distinct.
- The personal account home page and header are more convenient on mobile phones, with improved layout and adaptation for small screens.
- The onboarding wizard now redirects to the home page—"workspace"—after completion, rather than to the list of process models, making it easier to continue working.
- Table and card rendering in blog articles has been improved on mobile devices, and images are now sharper on retina screens and lighter in size on phones.
- Reports now handle expanded charts better: focus remains within the window, simplifying keyboard navigation.  

## 🔧 Fixes {#🔧-исправления}

- Improved the stability of AI-generated BPMN diagrams: instead of critical errors, the model correctly regenerates the result.  
- Fixed specific visual artifacts in the diagram editor: controls no longer drift outside the boundaries, the palette is no longer obscured by the header, and a clear loading indicator is displayed.  
- Resolved an issue where working with multiple diagrams on the canvas could display an incorrect schema or an outdated report version.  
- Improved the catalog functionality and emblem display in the “Achievements” section to ensure all rewards are correctly shown in the personal account.  
- Roadmap cards now display all user ideas and votes, with no hidden entries.  
- Fixed specific inaccuracies in the display of links and answer cards in tasks and surveys.
