## ✨ New Features {#✨-новые-возможности}

- The **Process Simulation Report** now includes new tabs: "Scenarios", "Buffers", "SLA", and "Configuration" — allowing you to analyze simulation results across different dimensions, identify bottlenecks, time reserves, and experiment settings in a single window.
- The **BPMN AI Assistant** now supports a "streaming" response toggle: you can either view the complete result immediately or observe the response being generated in parts — convenient for both quick estimates and detailed analysis.
- The **process registry** now provides explicit notifications about the automatic creation and detachment of BPMN models — making it easier to track which diagrams are linked to which records and what occurred during changes.
- In the **BPMN editor**, clicking on the "chip" displaying the diagram type now navigates to the associated diagram — navigation between linked models has become faster and more intuitive.
- The **wiki** now features a full draft mode: new pages are initially created as invisible drafts, supporting autosave and a separate draft title, while published content is additionally cleaned of unnecessary attachments — this reduces the risk of "raw" publications and text loss.
- **Version history** with a side panel and improved table of contents has been added for wiki pages: you can view changes chronologically, navigate the structure of long documents more easily, and quickly jump to specific sections.
- A **fixed "pin panel" and 1:1 preview mode of the published version** have been introduced in wiki edit mode — this helps you see how the page will appear to readers without distraction from service elements.
- In the wiki "Published" mode, the header, menu, author display, and "breadcrumbs" have been redesigned — making it easier for users to understand the page context, its author, and its place within the knowledge base structure.
- The **"Folders"** section has improved search and move functionality: added highlighting of found text, icons and a close button in the move window, confirmation for bulk actions, and search within the tree during transfer — this reduces errors and speeds up work with large structures.
- A **portfolio dashboard** (portfolio) has been introduced: dedicated pages and menus for managing the process portfolio, a catalog tree, and filtering by owner — this forms the foundation for a managerial view of the company's process set.
- The **events** section now includes an event feed and a /events page, as well as an "Add to Calendar" button (Google, Outlook, .ics) and a backup link to the Telegram community — making it easier to track events and avoid missing them.
- Marketing pages now feature a **lead magnet "BPMN Cheat Sheet"** and redesigned BPMN element pages (facts, contextual hints, "other elements in group" block) — helping you master the notation faster and select the right elements for your task.
- **Pricing and product/role pages** have been updated: a calculator and scenario toggle have been added, pricing cards and product description blocks have been improved — making it easier to choose the right plan and understand how STORM BPMN addresses the needs of a specific role.

## 🚀 Improvements {#🚀-улучшения}

- The simulation report now features a **new structure with 10 tabs and convenient "pill" toggles**, along with a unified number format that accounts for locale settings. This makes the information easier to read and simplifies comparing metrics.
- In the **AI chat**, handling of cancelled responses has been improved: the system now reacts more accurately to generation interruptions and avoids unnecessary actions on diagrams, making the assistant's behavior more predictable.
- In the **process registry**, the display of parent entries and the filter by owner has been improved. This makes it easier to understand the hierarchy and quickly find specific processes by their responsible parties.
- In the **"Folders"** section, the tree interface and bulk operations have been refined: drag-and-drop indicators, text selection, styling for destructive actions, and breadcrumb behavior have been improved. This reduces the likelihood of accidental errors and makes navigation more intuitive.
- In the **wiki**, the table of contents, sidebar, and navigation tree have been improved: the "Contents" header is now pinned, clicking on lines works like in modern editors (Notion-style), and scenarios for moving, renaming, and deleting items have been redesigned. Navigating large articles and the knowledge base structure is now significantly more convenient.
- In wiki edit mode, smooth "typewriter-style" scrolling has been added, and synchronization between the draft and the published version has been improved. Editing long texts is now more comfortable.
- In the **folders and move interface**, the bulk bar has been simplified, icons and a close button have been added for quick dismissal, and search has become more intuitive. Bulk operations now take less time.
- In the **admin activity section** and other internal interface areas, the UI has been standardized to use a unified style and common components. This increases behavioral predictability and lowers the barrier to entry for new users.
- On the product website, the **contact-sales** forms, footer, fonts, buttons, and section navigation have been improved. It is now easier for potential clients to contact the team and find the necessary information.
- Marketing pages (home, pricing, product and role pages, implementation guide, and BPMN element pages) have received an updated design and improved structure. Information about STORM BPMN capabilities is now more visual and coherent.
- The event feed and event lightbox have been refined for usability: focus management works correctly, unnecessary scrolling has been eliminated, and loading error states are clear. Viewing events is now more stable and comfortable.

- In the **process registry** and BPMN model management, issues with detaching and moving models, as well as owner filters, have been resolved — data is displayed correctly and is not lost during changes.
