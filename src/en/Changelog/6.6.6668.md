## ⚠️ Changes for Enterprise clients {#⚠️-изменения-для-enterprise-клиентов}

### Stricter settings for unauthenticated users {#настроики-для-неавторизованных-пользователеи-отдаются-строже}

As part of vulnerability remediation, the rules for anonymous delivery of global settings (which affect the login screen and the license page) have been changed:

-   Unauthenticated users now receive **only** settings with the **"Visibility setting"** toggle explicitly enabled. Previously, a setting with visibility unchecked could also be included in the response.
-   Settings containing "secret" substrings in their names (`secret`, `token`, `password`, `smtp`, `ldap`, etc.) are **never** delivered to anonymous users, even if visibility is enabled.

**What to check after the update:** whether visibility is enabled for the login screen settings — `showBasicLogin`, `showCustomLoginButtonName`, `CustomLoginButtonName`, `enableYandexSSO`, `showRegistraton`, `customMenuItems`, `licensePageHtml`. If the login form has "disappeared," open the login page with the `?showBasicLogin=true` parameter and correct the visibility in the admin panel.

Details: [Settings visibility for unauthenticated users](/en/configure/README.md#видимость-настроек-для-неавторизованных-пользователеи).

### Update checklist {#чеклист-обновления}
