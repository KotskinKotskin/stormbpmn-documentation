## ✨ New features {#✨-новые-возможности}

- A new process view mode by lane (swimlanes) has been introduced: you can now clearly see who is responsible for what in the process in a convenient "matrix" view, without the risk of accidentally changing anything.
- Extended editor settings for teams have been added: you can restrict the use of individual BPMN elements, set default values, and work in an updated, more intuitive settings interface.
- Routable product learning tours and a business banner for trial accounts have been introduced — new users can more easily get acquainted with the core capabilities of STORM BPMN directly within the interface.
- Automatic detection of the company logo and brand color when creating a team has been implemented: the workspace appearance now automatically adapts to the brand without manual configuration.
- Named seat management has been added for HYBRID licenses: administrators can flexibly distribute access among employees and monitor license usage.

## 🚀 Improvements {#🚀-улучшения}

- The "Command Processes" page has received a visual update: it is now easier to navigate the process list and find the required items.  
- The diagram editor has been redesigned around the "infinite canvas" concept with floating islands: working with large process models is now more convenient, and the structure of large processes is clearer.  
- Scaling and navigation in the editor have been improved (including control via Ctrl + / -), smart copy-paste has been refined, and the appearance of multiple selection has been updated — this speeds up everyday work with diagrams.  
- Diagram viewing in "read-only" mode has become more polished: custom text styles are displayed correctly, and tooltips show the author and the time of the last update.  
- Parsing of BPMN models and interaction with the local browser storage have been optimized: large diagrams open faster and more stably, especially during extended use and in multiple tabs.  
- The billing and payment interface has been updated, including for the dark theme: the design is now more consistent and user-friendly for those working with billing.  
- The appearance of preview pop-ups, headers, and tags (including dark mode), as well as specific interface elements in team settings and overlays, has been improved — the interface is now cleaner and visually more stable.  
- The translation system for the processes section has been updated for English and German: the text is now clearer and more accurate for international teams.

## 🔧 Fixes {#🔧-исправления}

- Improved the stability of payments and billing: reduced the number of errors during payment processing and auto-completion of account details.  
- Fixed specific issues in the editor: preview now works correctly, the context menu no longer flickers, predictable zoom behavior has been restored, and the positioning of tooltips has been improved.  
- Resolved rare errors when opening regulations in embedded mode and when accessing diagram views via links, including for authenticated users.  
- Enhanced the reliability of system logout: eliminated cascading errors that could interfere with proper logoff.  
- Fixed interface display issues in dark theme (headers, tags, specific panels) to ensure all elements remain readable and neat.  
- Refined level-based filters in the registry and other search elements: results are now displayed correctly and predictably.  
- Improved the robustness of local storage (IndexedDB) operations: outdated data is cleared, conflicts between tabs are handled correctly, reducing the risk of unexpected failures.  
- Corrected minor inaccuracies in the interface of marketing forms, comments, and headers to ensure forms and lists display without artifacts or unnecessary messages.
