## ✨ New Features {#✨-новые-возможности}

- **Interactive DES process simulator**: Configurable buffers (interoperational storage), probabilities at decision points (XOR gateways), and convenient settings overlays directly on the diagram have been added. This allows for more accurate modeling of real queues, bottlenecks, and process flow variations without navigating complex settings.

- **AI assistant for simulation configuration**: An AI-based generator for DES simulation configurations has been added. You describe the process and the goal, and the system suggests ready-made settings for modeling, saving time for analysts and process owners.

- **Full-featured lane (swimlane) support in BPMN**: Lanes are now a separate diagram element with editing capabilities, geometry configuration, and chat-based discussion. This simplifies visual responsibility separation and collaborative work on the process model.

- **Diagram element search by exact ID**: You can quickly find a specific element by its identifier. This is convenient when working with large models and when coordinating via links to specific objects.

- **Convenient org structure navigation**: When navigating via a link to a specific org unit, it is automatically focused and highlighted. This speeds up the analysis of complex org structures and link sharing between colleagues.

- **Extended employee catalog**: Search and sorting have been added to the employee list. It is now easier to find a specific person in large organizations.

- **Wiki: Reusing page fragments**: You can embed parts of one wiki page into another. This helps maintain a single text across multiple sections without duplication and reduces the risk of discrepancies in documentation.

- **Wiki: Word-level version comparison**: Version comparison now shows differences at the word level with neat column alignment. This facilitates change reviews and finding exact edits in texts.

- **Onboarding: AI-first tour**: The interactive product tour has been updated with a focus on AI features and more prominent highlighting of key interface areas. New users can more easily understand where and how to use STORM's AI capabilities.

- **Marketing quests and learning scenarios**: A new "analytical process" scenario has been added, and game mechanics have been expanded (interactive mini-BPMN stages with a timer, an archetype gallery and "secret endings," and a contact form at the end). This provides more opportunities for user training and engagement in "game + process thinking" formats.

- **Multilingual marketing site and help (ru/en/de)**: Key pages for authentication, help, implementation guides, content series, and the DES quiz have been translated and are managed through a unified localization system. This simplifies work for international teams and partners.

## 🚀 Improvements {#🚀-улучшения}

- **Working with private models and folders**: Improved saving of private models to the required folders and added clickability to the folder "chip" in the models table, allowing direct navigation into the folder. This makes navigation through the model repository more predictable and faster.

- **BPMN diagrams and comments**: The open comment form no longer overlaps adjacent elements, and navigating links in read-only mode no longer resets focus. Discussing and viewing complex diagrams has become more comfortable.

- **Org structure and roles**: Improved the logic of connections between org units and the display of the manager in the role card. The company structure has become clearer for business users.

- **Filters in process tables**: Selecting a value in a filter no longer automatically closes the menu. This speeds up the configuration of complex filters and the selection of required data slices.

- **Custom fields and key metrics**: Deleting a custom field now automatically clears associated key metrics. This reduces the risk of "junk" metrics and confusion in reports.

- **Wiki: stability and convenience when working with drafts**: Improved protection against version conflicts, neat diffing of containers, and a limit on the length of change comments. Working with documentation has become more reliable during active collaborative editing.

- **Team settings and accessibility**: Selection of overly light team theme colors is now prohibited, and keyboard navigation for tooltips has been improved. The interface has become more readable and accessible for all users.

- **Command palette (system-wide search)**: Improved hotkey behavior (they are no longer intercepted in text fields, search cancellations are handled correctly, and results no longer "jump"). Quick search for STORM objects has become more stable and predictable.

- **DES simulation interface**: Task, buffer, and gateway settings are now available directly on the canvas, and resource and "no resource" badges are displayed more clearly and always when relevant. It is easier for analysts to identify problem areas in the process and quickly adjust parameters.
