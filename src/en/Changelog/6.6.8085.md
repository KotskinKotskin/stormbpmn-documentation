## ✨ New features {#✨-новые-возможности}

- An extended achievement system has been introduced: 206 achievements, medal badges, ranks, and a merch program (a "Year with Storm" T-shirt, socks, and other prizes for activity and paid invoices). This helps reward the team for regular work in STORM and makes using the product more engaging.
- A "Lean Six Sigma" tab has been added to process model reports: waste analysis (8 types of Muda), constraint theory elements, and a dashboard with visual indicators. It is now possible to evaluate process efficiency not only by time and load but also by lean metrics.
- Overlays and node markers have appeared on the "Diagram" tab of the process simulation report. This allows you to quickly identify bottlenecks and key points directly on the diagram, without complex table analysis.
- A "Child processes" tab has been added to the process card in the process registry. It is now easier to see which sub-processes are linked to the main process and to manage complex chains.
- The wiki now allows restoring a page from version history. You can safely experiment with content and quickly roll back to a specific version if needed.
- A draft indicator is now displayed in the wiki tree. This helps distinguish published materials from working versions and prevents confusion during navigation.
- Process search now takes the process code (e.g., P2P.1.2) into account and uses an improved search mechanism. Finding a specific process by internal code or a fragment of the description has become easier and more accurate.
- The embedded diagram view (embed) for free authors has received an AI promo banner. This provides users with more information about STORM's AI tool capabilities directly within embedded diagrams.
- A "Document with Table of Contents" block and updated typography with a new logo have appeared on marketing pages. Product materials have become more structured and easier to read.
- The "Tell us about your case" button now leads to the cases page on the website, rather than an external messenger. This simplifies knowledge sharing and allows users to share STORM usage stories in a more familiar format.

## 🚀 Improvements {#🚀-улучшения}

- The achievements showcase is now simpler and more intuitive: the "level ladder" above the showcase has been removed, product cards no longer require unnecessary scrolling, and a tidy toolbar replaces the "floating" buttons. Users can more easily understand which prizes are available and how to earn them.
- The wiki is now more convenient and secure: the status of unpublished pages is displayed correctly, the drafts list is synchronized, the tree loads "lazily" (faster opening), and fragment extraction is possible from drafts as well. This reduces the risk of version confusion and speeds up work with large volumes of knowledge.
- Wiki permission management has been improved: actions on records now strictly depend on permissions, readers are guaranteed access to permitted materials, and clear notifications are displayed when permissions change. This makes collaboration predictable and transparent.
- Wiki version restoration and draft handling have become more reliable: unsaved changes are not lost when leaving a page, and autosave no longer "rolls back" published versions. This reduces the risk of losing important edits.
- Process search has been improved: results take into account the recency of changes and update correctly when process structures change. Users see the current state of processes in search results.
- Diagram and report display has been improved: tooltips on diagrams work correctly (for example, when hovering over diagram segments), legends and element boundaries have been refined. Analysis of visual reports has become clearer.
- The registration and login experience has been improved: more reliable handling of incorrect data, protection against duplicate form submissions, and correct handling of team invitations and license seats. This reduces the number of errors when onboarding new users.
- The screen suggesting an upgrade to a paid plan when the team grows has been improved: a threshold of 5 colleagues is taken into account, the warning persists longer, and the limitations are explained more clearly. It is easier for managers to plan team expansion in STORM.
- The wiki, marketing pages, and widgets have been improved in the dark theme: background colors, borders, version history display, sidebar, editor, and images have been fixed. Working in the dark interface has become more comfortable and visually consistent.
- Subscription and invoice descriptions, as well as the site's SEO structure (section addresses, cross-linking between Stormverse, registries, and BPMN elements), have been improved. This helps users navigate pricing plans faster and find relevant product materials more easily.
- The built-in editor (tiptap) handles embedded materials (iframe) and author content more carefully. This reduces the risk of accidental restrictions or loss of embedded elements.  

## 🔧 Fixes {#🔧-исправления}

- Improved the stability of the achievements system: adjusted the conditions for certain achievements, removed the outdated "course" achievement, and enhanced the reliability of calculations for paid time and invoices. Users receive correct rewards without failures.
- Resolved issues in the process registry related to list updates, scrolling, and the child processes tab. Viewing and navigating complex process structures has become predictable.
- Fixed situations in the wiki where drafts and access rights behaved inconsistently (statuses, saves, reader access). This eliminated rare but unpleasant cases of lost or "missing" changes.
- Fixed errors in the embed view of diagrams when switching diagrams within a single page. Embedded diagrams now update correctly without reloading.
- Fixed the behavior of custom fields: re-editing no longer resets default values. Form settings remain as you configured them.

- Fixed minor inaccuracies in localization and wording (declensions, headings, brand). The interface appears more polished and consistent across different languages.
- Resolved issues on marketing pages related to redirects, 404 pages, meta tags, and structured data. Link transitions are now more reliable, and pages display correctly in search engines.
