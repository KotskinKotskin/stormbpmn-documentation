## ✨ New Features {#✨-новые-возможности}

- A detailed guide on process optimization (DES Optimizations Guide) is now available: pages with examples, problem indicators, and real-world stories help you quickly identify where and how to improve a process.
- The optimizations list page now includes "Optimizations" and "Glossary" tabs, as well as a block with similar optimizations and terms — making it easier to navigate and improve the process systematically.
- Export of the org structure to Excel has been added — you can now export the org structure for reports, approvals, and work outside the system.
- Optimization cards now feature new "Problem Indicators" and "Real-World Story" sections — this helps you quickly understand when to apply a specific optimization and what to expect in practice.
- A calendar has been added for roles in processes — making it easier to plan availability and distribute tasks among participants.  

## 🚀 Improvements {#🚀-улучшения}

- The optimizations page has been redesigned in a unified style with BPMN elements: information is now more structured, cards are more visual, and navigation is simpler.  
- The glossary has been improved: a two-column format, a separate tab, and a "Getting Started" block help you understand terms faster and avoid getting lost in the list.  
- For each optimization, only the related glossary terms are now displayed — less "noise" and more value in the context of a specific task.  
- The process save window opens faster, and selecting subprocesses (Call Activity) has become more convenient thanks to an improved dropdown list.  
- The "To Top" button has been moved higher and is no longer obscured by the chat, while search fields on pages are now full-width and located within their respective tabs — working with long lists is now more comfortable.  
- When zooming the org structure, the head of the organizational unit is displayed more clearly, and a "Parent" field has been added to tables — making it easier to understand the hierarchy.  

## 🔧 Fixes {#🔧-исправления}

- Improved authorization handling, including in pop-up windows, and ensured that tariff information is correctly reset upon system logout.  
- Clarified and translated the names of optimization categories and elements (including experts and gateways) into Russian, removed internal technical codes, and eliminated references to "free" in the interface.  
- Fixed icon rendering, tab bottom borders, tooltip positioning, and minor visual issues on the optimizations and glossary pages.  
- Enhanced validation of uploaded files and schemas, as well as guest search functionality — search results are preserved even after a guest is deleted.  
- Resolved specific issues with goal selection in role duplication and EA windows, org structure display, and additional information in feedback forms.
